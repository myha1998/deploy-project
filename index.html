What happens on a visit

Five steps, the same order, every time

No two pools need the same water. The process does not change.

  1. 01

    We arrive in the window we gave you

    Same day, same crew. If that changes, you hear it from us first.

  2. 02

    Test the water before touching it

    Free chlorine, pH, alkalinity, stabiliser, clarity. Numbers first, chemicals second.

  3. 03

    Clean the pool

    Skim the surface, brush the walls and steps, vacuum the floor, empty the baskets.

  4. 04

    Balance what needs balancing

    Only what the test calls for. Not a set dose out of a jug.

  5. 05

    Leave the note

    Date, time, water status, and the work performed. On your door before we drive away.

For commercial pools, those same records are what a health inspector asks to see — and Virginia requires them to be kept for a year.

Why Virginia pools need a schedule

This is not Arizona water

Virginia summers are humid and stormy, which means pollen and debris in spring, algae the moment circulation drops in July, and leaves through October. A pool left alone for two weeks does not just look bad — it costs real money to bring back.

We are local, and the crew is the same one every week. If the pump is getting loud or the chemistry is drifting, you hear it from us before you notice it yourself.

Questions

The things people ask before they book

Do I need to be home during service?

No. Most customers are at work. We need a way into the pool area — a gate code, a side-yard path, or a lockbox — and a door to leave the note on. If a dog is in the yard, tell us once and we will work around it.

How often should a pool be serviced?

Weekly for most pools in use through the summer. Bi-weekly works for a small pool with light use, or a pool under a cover. In the middle of a Virginia July, weekly is not upselling — it is the interval at which the water stays stable.

What is included in a maintenance visit?

Skim the surface, brush the walls and steps, vacuum the floor, empty the skimmer and pump baskets, test the water, and balance what the test calls for. Then a written note with the date, time, water status, and work done.

How much does pool service cost?

It depends on pool size, how much debris the yard drops, and how often you want us. The packages above are priced from a monthly rate, and seasonal open and close are quoted separately.

We would rather show you a number and explain it than hand you “call for pricing.”

Do you service commercial pools?

Yes — hotels, motels, gyms, apartment complexes, HOAs, swim clubs, parks, and other regulated public pools. The owner holds the Certified Pool Operator certification that Prince William County requires on site, and the water log is kept inspection-ready.

Do you repair all brands?

Yes. Pumps, motors, heaters, filters, lights, timers, and automation across the major brands. If a part is discontinued we will tell you what the replacement options are before ordering anything.

What is the difference between weekly and bi-weekly?

Only the interval. The visit itself is identical — same cleaning, same testing, same written note. Weekly gets you more frequent chemistry correction, which matters most in July and August.

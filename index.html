Why Virginia pool owners

Virginia weather is tough on pools.

Hot, humid summers, heavy storms, and seasonal debris cause algae, unbalanced water, equipment strain, and buildup that damage your pool and drag down curb appeal. Manassas Pool Service provides professional pool care designed for the local Virginia climate — from routine maintenance and water balancing to equipment checks and full-service care.

The difference you will notice first is the note. After every visit we leave a written record: the date, the time, the water status, and exactly what we did. Most companies do not do this. It is the reason a lot of our customers switched.

For property managers

Your pool must have a certified operator on site while it's open. Ours is the owner.

In Prince William County, commercial pools are required to have a professionally trained Certified Pool Operator on site when the pool is in operation, with water quality tested and posted. We do not sell pool cleaning to a property manager. We sell inspection readiness.

Questions

The things people ask before they book

Do I need to be home during service?
No. As long as we have access to your backyard and equipment pad, we complete the service and leave the written note behind.
How often should a pool be serviced?
Most pools do best on weekly service. Bi-weekly works for pools with lighter use. We will recommend a schedule after a first visit.
What is included in a maintenance visit?
Bottom vacuuming, top skimming, pump emptying, chemical balancing, wall brushing, and a written note with date, time, water status, and work done.
Do you service commercial pools?
Yes. Apartments, HOAs, hotels, motels, gyms, swim clubs, parks, and regulated public pools. The owner is a Certified Pool Operator, which is required for commercial pools in this county.
Do you repair all equipment brands?
Yes. Lights, motors, timers, heaters, filters, pumps, tiles, skimmers, leaks, drains, and automation — all brands.
What is the difference between weekly and bi-weekly?
Frequency. Both include the same testing, cleaning, and written note. Weekly gives you more frequent chemistry control.
